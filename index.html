About Us

Industrial Excellence Since 1968

For over five decades, Riverstick Industries has supplied premium industrial and landscaping materials from our base in Curra, Riverstick, Co. Cork. From bark mulch to heavy-duty rubber flooring, we help our customers get the job done right.

Our commitment to quality, reliability and customer service has made us the preferred choice for contractors, landscapers and facility managers throughout Ireland and beyond.

  • Built to perform, designed to last — materials chosen for durability and real-world performance.
  • Locally sourced & processed — proudly Cork-based, supplying customers across the country.
  • Expert advice & support — a knowledgeable team ready to help you choose the right product.
